Actual corrective mechanism
----------------------------
ZC26 KgpOutputQueue::lockDequeue returns one software slot and immediately takes
waiting producer wake after unlocking queue, while IWX consumer still holds main
workloop gate. A concurrent producer may refill one slot, fail its gate attempt,
fill/refuse again and rearm waiting for the next slot. This source-permitted
amplification is finite/ring bounded, not a proven lost wake or pool leak.

ZC28 adds a retained single-use queue service token at the EXISTING IWX gated
consumer entry, before mapped/conventional dispatch. takeWake leaves the pending
software-capacity obligation armed while serviceDepth>0. Outermost end rechecks
CURRENT open/capacity/waiter/owner under SAME queue lock, retains interface, drops
lock and signals once. Token is cleared BEFORE wake/reentry and releases its
queue after signaling. No lock is held across packet service or a stack callback.
Nested/reentrant service scopes balance on all returns; caller frame supplies
RAII lifetime. Stop/flush closes pull, clears waiting and advances reservation
epoch; outstanding tokens retain only queue, never packet/node. Detach can clear
owner and release public queue reference; token remains valid until balanced end.
The main-gated public queue lookup uses existing interface/detach contract, not
permission for arbitrary nongated concurrent q->queue destruction.

THIS IS NARROWER THAN FULL ZC12:
- outputStart hardware-oactive refusal remains byte-exact26.
- ALL positive completion low-water restarts/signalOutputThread remain exact26.
- no frontend bypass, packet-admission policy, ring size, batching, ACK priority,
  credit ordering, new timer or worker. Only timing of software-capacity wake.
- at scope-end oactive may refuse producer; existing later positive low-water
  completion clears/restarts/signals it. No completion obligation is suppressed.
- no ZC23 feedback cookie scope, whole-notification handoff or early-credit port.
- other HALs never enter this scope; IWM/IWN immediate behavior remains (depth0).

Expected effect: reduce repeated cross-thread wake/gate-retry work and tiny
producer refills during a finite consumer pass, preserving useful bounded host
admission. Actual-source model:32unscoped wakes vs1scoped with identical packet
ownership/progression. This is a corrective scheduling hypothesis, NOT proof
of lower kernel cost, restored throughput or complete incident causality.
