Historical AX201 reports retain their older build/setup and actual-mode
limits. Luiz's AX211 Alpha 2/P8R1 return has 42/42 mapped tests; 80 MHz is KGP's
assignment, not an independent 160 MHz capture. Older ZC5/7/8 system-AppleVTD
records did not by themselves prove driver Mode 2. None validates 2.0.0 HE/BF
on AX201/AX211. Informally called “P8R1” recent BE200 comparisons were actually
P10-VHT; the genuine older Alpha 2/P8R1 dataset stays distinct.

[All P10/R9 campaigns used for 2.0.0](results/2.0.0.md). Separate AWDL/BLE
experiments and analysis were not integrated into the 2.0.0 source.
