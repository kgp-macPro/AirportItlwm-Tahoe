AirportItlwm-Tahoe — Intel Wi-Fi with AppleVTD/IOMMU and an AWDL baseline on macOS Tahoe

AirportItlwm-Tahoe is an independent Intel Wi-Fi driver based on the
OpenIntelWireless/itlwm foundation and DexterSLamb's implementation. The installed
bundle remains AirportItlwm.kext. It uses KGP's restored legacy wireless
environment; it is not a complete native Continuity implementation.

QUALIFIED 1.0.0 RELEASE
The frozen 1.0.0 release is qualified on KGP's AX210 / PCI 8086:2725 setup under
Tahoe 26.6.2 (25G83), with scoped additional 26.7 evidence. Qualification covers
infrastructure Wi-Fi, DHCP/traffic, recovery cycles and the recorded AWDL/P2P,
AirPlay and Screen Mirroring baseline. Runtime captures and user-observed
results are distinguished in the release documentation. This is not universal
Intel/platform qualification. AirDrop, Continuity Camera, Personal Hotspot and
BE200 remain outside the qualified feature set.

The stable 1.0.0 release, installation requirements, exact release identity,
source/build records and credits remain available at:
https://github.com/kgp-macPro/AirportItlwm-Tahoe
The original forensic references below describe that frozen 1.0.0 release;
they are not identities or performance certificates for experimental beta builds.
