History: the no-system_profiler comparison corrected a real KGP TCP-TX-P1
measurement perturbation. Cached TX-control work around ZC13 enabled roughly
480–490 Mbps TX. Later TX ordering, RX-buffer exchange, lifecycle/BA work and
the private cached construction/device-facing control separation were retained
and reconciled through ZC19/22/26. Changing the wired M1 MacBook peer adapter
from Realtek RTL8156 to ASIX AX88179B coincided with much better RX; those old
and new cohorts must not be treated as an unchanged environment.

Known adverse behavior: ZC26 had a severe conventional collapse of both TCP/UDP
directions and recovered before the user's abort. That remains unresolved.
ZC28 coalesces software-queue capacity wakes during IWX send service, retains
hardware-completion wakes and omits frequent ZC27 successful-packet observation.
Codex reports 49/49 checks and one Release build; physical benefit is not proven.
