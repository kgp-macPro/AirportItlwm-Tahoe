## Test context and limits

- KGP reports a Mercusys MR27BE AP, VHT160/802.11ac, and a final Wi-Fi menu reading of channel 44, 160 MHz, 1732 Mbit/s PHY and WPA2. The archive independently supports channel 44/160 MHz at its boundaries; it does not retain the menu view or a continuous per-test PHY/mode trace. The 1732 Mbit/s PHY value is not iperf goodput.
- KGP reports the M1 MacBook Pro as the wired server, with its OWC Ethernet link limited to 1 Gbit/s; MBP Wi-Fi and Hack Ethernet were off. The archive records the server IP and scoped en2 route, but does not independently pin the peer model, OWC link rate or both interface-off states.
- KGP separately reports a manual 1→10→1-Gbit/s OWC server-link comparison with the other VHT160 setup unchanged: 10-second, single-stream TCP receiver goodput was Hack TX/RX 652/919 Mbit/s before, 602/368 Mbit/s at 10 Gbit/s, and 633/919 Mbit/s after restoration. The RX tests had 0, 608 and 0 sender retransmissions, respectively, over the full runs. These manual runs are **not** part of the 60-test archive or tables and have no independently audited raw data here. The RX drop is repeatedly associated with the 10-Gbit/s server-link configuration and disappears after restoration; its cause is not localized. Neither an adapter defect nor a driver fault or its exclusion is proven. The published 3×20 campaign used the 1-Gbit/s configuration only.
- The driver is unchanged Alpha 2/P8R1, so differences from older results cannot be attributed to a driver switch; AP, server and link setup changed. The 1-Gbit/s server link limits interpretation near its ceiling. The separately recorded high-rate UDP TX ceiling also coincides with high **client iperf process** CPU and does not identify the TCP RX/10-Gbit/s cause.
- `networkQuality` completed separately on en2: 64.13 Mbit/s download and 39.89 Mbit/s upload, an Internet result rather than the LAN iperf capacity. **BE200 VHT160 verified; AX201/AX210/AX211 VHT160 validation pending.** This campaign does not establish HE, native AirDrop, broader stability or another card’s performance. The [separate static AX VHT160 source review](README.md#80211acvht160-status-for-alpha-2p8r1) does not replace an AX hardware result.
